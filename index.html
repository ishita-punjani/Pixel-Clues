    How to play

    1. Solve a clue. Type the answer and press Enter.
    2. Earn pixels. A 6-letter answer gives you 6 pixels.
    3. Look at the reference image on the right, then draw directly on the grid: click a cell to place a pixel, click it again to remove it, or click-and-drag to paint or erase several at once.
    4. Use exactly that many pixels, all connected edge-to-edge (corners don't count), then press Confirm Shape.
    5. Rebuild the whole picture. Use Undo or Restart if you get stuck.
